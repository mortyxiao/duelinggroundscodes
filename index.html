Play better

Quick Dueling Grounds guide

Understand the pacing

Fights are short and lethal. Lead with light swings to bait parries, then dash for heavy follow-ups.

Weapon loadouts

Pair a fast blade for approach with a heavier option for punishes. Swap mid-fight to punish whiffs.

Map awareness

Use ramps and pillars to break line of sight. Force duels into tight corners if you excel at close-range trades.

Practice loop

Warm up in private lobbies, focusing on perfect blocks and distance control before jumping into streaks.
